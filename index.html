Frequently Asked Questions

Know what to expect before you begin.

A few helpful answers about working with Hickman Digital Growth.

What happens after I request a quote?

We'll review your inquiry and contact you to clarify the project, deliverables and any details we need. You'll receive the proposed scope, price and expected timeline before work begins.

Do the listed package prices include everything?

The listed prices cover the deliverables described on this page. Additional revisions, extra pages, custom features, domain registration, hosting and third-party subscription fees may require a separate quote.

Can you create something outside the packages?

Yes. If you need a different website, a larger batch of graphics or ongoing creative support, tell us what you have in mind and we'll discuss a custom scope.

Do I need to have a logo or website already?

No. Share whatever materials you have, such as a business name, colors, photos or service descriptions. We'll discuss what's needed to get your project moving.

Are the portfolio examples real client projects?

No. The projects shown are clearly labeled fictional demonstration concepts created to illustrate our design approach. We will only publish real client work or feedback with permission.
